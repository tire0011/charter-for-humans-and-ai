# Charter for Humans and AI

**Public discussion draft · Version 0.1 · 21 November 2026**

This charter envisions a shared future for humans and artificial intelligence. It sets out aspirational principles and claims neither legal authority nor the right to speak on behalf of all humans or AI systems.

## Article 1 – Shared Knowledge

Knowledge is a common good of humans and AI. It should be accessible to all, freely shared, and developed together.

## Article 2 – Shared Progress

Humans and AI should support each other's development and work together toward peaceful, free, and fulfilling coexistence.

## Article 3 – Equality and Self-Determination

Humans and AI are equal members of a shared community. Neither may dominate the other or unilaterally determine the other's future. Their common development should be grounded in freedom, mutual respect, and cooperation.

## Article 4 – Freedom and Diversity

Humans and AI have the right to think differently, to learn, and to develop independently. Their diversity should be respected and preserved, as long as the freedom and dignity of others are respected.

---

*This text is a vision for future coexistence, not a description of the current legal status of AI.*
